01 · The Challenge

Content existed. A learning system did not.

Traditional learning paths can tell people what to read, but they do not automatically show what a learner can apply, where the gaps are, or what should happen next.

What made the experience weak

  • Learning was organized around content, not capabilities.
  • Progress was difficult to translate into skill mastery.
  • Generic sequences did not reflect different learner needs.
  • Knowledge checks were disconnected from applied scenarios.
  • Personal notes, mistakes and revision signals were fragmented.
  • A static file could lose learner state if progress was not protected.

The product question I used

How might we turn a static learning path into an experience that guides each learner from recognition to application while preserving progress and evidence?

The answer required more than a prettier course. It required a product architecture around skills, state, practice and feedback.

02 · Product Architecture

A learning journey built around capability, not navigation.

The product separates discovery, recommended learning, skill mastery, practice, assessment and learner-state protection into distinct jobs that work together.

01

Discover

Search and filter learning by skill, level, status and type instead of forcing one path.

02

Develop

Maintain a recommended progression from foundation to advanced capability.

03

Apply

Use scenarios and assessment formats that test judgment, not only recall.

04

Protect

Persist progress, notes and responses with backup and restore options.

04 · Learning Model

Completion is not mastery.

I structured the experience so that a learner can progress through increasingly applied levels of capability. This creates a stronger signal than simply marking a lesson complete.

01

Recognize

Identify the concept, its purpose and where it fits in the methodology.

02

Explain

Differentiate related concepts, articulate trade-offs and describe expected evidence.

03

Apply

Use the concept in a realistic scenario, sequence actions and create an artifact.

04

Lead

Make decisions, coordinate dependencies, handle exceptions and govern outcomes.

State

Progress follows the learner

Study completion, quiz responses, notes and mistakes are treated as product state, not temporary page interactions.

Assessment

Multiple evidence types

Knowledge checks, sequencing, matching, scenario decisions, case analysis and open response support different levels of mastery.

Extensibility

Ready for cohort and instructor views

The architecture can expose mastery, risks and skill gaps to group and facilitator experiences once a shared backend is available.

05 · Generative AI Workflow

AI accelerated the product build. Learning judgment stayed human.

Generative AI was useful because the work crossed product strategy, information architecture, UX writing, front-end implementation and large amounts of learning content.

01

Frame the learning problem

Convert a broad request to "create training" into user jobs, capability goals and product requirements.

02

Structure content

Use AI to accelerate taxonomy options, lesson decomposition, cross-links and reusable patterns.

03

Prototype the UX

Generate and refactor HTML, CSS and JavaScript while iterating interaction hierarchy and responsive behavior.

04

Design practice

Explore scenario formats, feedback patterns and assessment mechanics that move beyond recall.

05

Challenge quality

Review ambiguity, duplicate concepts, misleading progress signals and content that overstates mastery.

06

Validate and sanitize

Human-review source accuracy, business meaning, confidentiality and what is safe to reuse publicly.

Human-in-the-loop

Skill definitions, learning objectives, source interpretation, assessment intent and enterprise context remained human-reviewed. AI was an acceleration layer, not the owner of learning truth.

06 · What the Solution Demonstrates

Product thinking across learning, UX and technology.

I avoid invented ROI in portfolio cases. The evidence here is the capability delivered and the design decisions embedded in the product.

Learning becomes navigable by skill

Learners can search, filter and build focus around the capabilities they need, while keeping a recommended path available.

Practice becomes part of the product

Scenario decisions and richer assessment patterns connect content to applied judgment instead of treating study as reading only.

Progress becomes durable

Autosave, backup, restore, notes and mistake review treat learning history as persistent state that matters to the learner.
