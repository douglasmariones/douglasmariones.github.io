01 · The Challenge

A license can be assigned in minutes. Confidence cannot.

New learners had to understand identity and access, navigate a large learning ecosystem, select the right path, interpret certification rules and know where to go when something failed.

Where friction accumulated

  • Identity, access and account concepts were easy to confuse.
  • Platform navigation required knowledge learners did not yet have.
  • Learning-path selection could produce too many options.
  • Certification preparation had rules and dependencies that changed over time.
  • Support requests often lacked the evidence needed for fast triage.
  • Information lived across portals, help pages, live sessions and personal guidance.

The product question I used

How might we reduce the distance between receiving learning access and confidently taking the next correct action?

The toolkit needed to be useful before the first course, during the learning journey and when a problem interrupted progress.

04 · Enablement Beyond the Toolkit

The product was paired with human facilitation.

A self-service experience works better when it complements live moments that create context, trust and shared language. I used digital and facilitator-led touchpoints together.

Kick-off

Start with the why and the journey

Live kick-offs oriented participants around program goals, how the learning ecosystem worked and what action to take first.

Bilingual delivery

English and Portuguese

Training and onboarding could be facilitated for multicultural audiences without requiring a separate product experience for every session.

Learning rituals

Follow-up, not one-time communication

Structured follow-up moments reinforced progress, surfaced friction and helped learners convert access into continued development.

01

Orientation

What is this program, why does it matter and what should I do first?

02

Navigation

Where do I find the right learning path, certification or help article?

03

Confidence

What rules matter, what is current and how do I validate information?

04

Support

How do I describe the issue, collect evidence and reach the right support path?

05 · Information Governance

Self-service only works when the content can be trusted.

The toolkit separates guidance from source-of-truth content. Time-sensitive rules should be validated against official sources instead of being frozen permanently into onboarding copy.

Searchable knowledge

Global search, category-based FAQs and a glossary reduce the need to know the platform's vocabulary before finding an answer.

Evidence-ready support

Ticket guidance asks for time, environment, expected result, actual result, steps to reproduce and attachments, improving triage quality.

Source-aware guidance

Critical certification and support information is framed as guidance that should be confirmed in authoritative sources when it can change.

06 · Generative AI Workflow

AI helped scale structure and iteration, not policy authority.

The toolkit involved a large amount of information, repeated learner questions, UX copy and front-end behavior. Generative AI accelerated synthesis and prototyping while human review remained responsible for accuracy and context.

01

Map learner friction

Turn recurring questions and onboarding issues into user jobs and decision points.

02

Design the information architecture

Explore grouping, navigation and search structures around user intent instead of source-system menus.

03

Synthesize self-service content

Draft FAQ, glossary and support patterns from reviewed materials, then simplify language for new learners.

04

Prototype interactions

Build and refine responsive HTML, CSS and JavaScript for search, navigation, filtering and support flows.

05

Review edge cases

Challenge ambiguous language, outdated assumptions and points where users could choose the wrong support route.

06

Keep authority explicit

Separate generated or summarized guidance from the official sources that govern time-sensitive decisions.

Human-in-the-loop

Certification rules, support procedures, program decisions, live facilitation and source validation remained human-reviewed. AI accelerated the product work without becoming the source of truth.

07 · What the Solution Demonstrates

Enablement is a product problem, not only a communications task.

I do not claim unverified time or adoption savings. The case demonstrates the product capabilities created to reduce ambiguity and support learner action.

A single learner entry point

Onboarding, learning-path selection, certification guidance and support are connected in one experience.

Self-service with escalation

The toolkit answers common questions but also makes escalation clearer when self-service is not enough.

Reusable enablement architecture

The pattern can support new cohorts, topics and enterprise platforms without rebuilding the onboarding model from zero.
